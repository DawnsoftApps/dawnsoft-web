Archives and collections

  • Archive: a snapshot of the tabs in one Safari window. Use it to save a browsing session and return to it later.
  • Collection: a long-term topic containing tabs selected from one or more archives. The same tab can belong to multiple collections.

Removing an archive or collection does not remove a tab that is still used elsewhere. Search can find tabs by title or URL and can find archives and collections by name.

Enable the Safari extension

iPhone or iPad: open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions, select Tabs Keeper, and turn it on. On older iOS versions, Safari may appear directly in Settings.

Mac: open Safari → Settings → Extensions and enable Tabs Keeper.

Safari asks for access so the extension can read the title and URL of tabs in the current window when you open it. Tabs Keeper does not read webpage text, form entries, passwords, or cookies. You can review or revoke access in Safari’s extension settings at any time.

Save open tabs

  1. Open the Safari window whose tabs you want to save.
  2. Choose Tabs Keeper from Safari’s extensions menu.
  3. Review the tabs and deselect anything you do not want to keep.
  4. Save the selection as an archive.

Only valid web links using HTTP or HTTPS are saved. Open an archive in the extension or app to reopen one tab or the entire archive.

Organize tabs into a collection

Open the Tabs Keeper app, create a collection, and add tabs from your saved archives. You can search the available tabs, select them manually, and later add or remove tabs without changing the original archive.

Use AI Smart Select

On supported Apple Intelligence devices, AI Smart Select can suggest tabs for a collection based on the collection name, tab titles, and domains. Processing uses Apple’s on-device system language model. It does not read full webpage content or automatically add, remove, or delete tabs. Review the suggestions and confirm your selection before importing.

If the button is not shown, the device, operating system, language model, or current Apple Intelligence settings do not support this feature. You can always select tabs manually.

Free and Pro features

The free version can keep up to 2 active archives and 1 active collection. Tabs Keeper Pro removes those limits and enables iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Available plans can include monthly, annual, and lifetime purchases; prices and availability are shown by the App Store.

Set up iCloud sync

  1. Sign in to the same Apple Account on every device and make sure iCloud Drive is available.
  2. Confirm that Tabs Keeper Pro is active.
  3. Open Settings → Pro & Sync and turn on iCloud Sync.
  4. Allow the first sync to finish before making large changes on another device.

Sync is off by default and uses your private CloudKit database. Turning sync off, or losing Pro access, pauses syncing but does not delete existing local data. If an error appears, follow its instructions and choose Sync Again.

Restore purchases and manage subscriptions

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase, then open Settings → Pro & Sync and choose Restore Purchases. If the purchase still does not appear, check your network connection and the App Store purchase history before trying again.

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Use Manage Subscription in the Pro screen or your Apple Account subscription settings to change or cancel a plan. Buying lifetime access does not automatically cancel an existing subscription; cancel that subscription separately to avoid another renewal.

Delete or restore content

Deleted archives and collections stay in Settings → Trash for 7 days. You can restore or permanently delete them during that time. Expired items are permanently removed the next time the app opens. Permanently deleted items cannot be recovered by Tabs Keeper.

Restoring content must stay within the free-version limit unless Pro is active. If iCloud sync is enabled, deletions and restorations are also synchronized.

Troubleshooting

The extension is missing: confirm that Tabs Keeper is installed, enable its Safari extension in system settings, then quit and reopen Safari.

Tabs do not save: confirm that the extension has access to the current website, that at least one HTTP or HTTPS tab is selected, and that the free archive limit has not been reached.

iCloud does not sync: confirm the Apple Account, iCloud Drive, Pro status, and iCloud Sync setting. Check for an iCloud storage or permission warning, then choose Sync Again.

A purchase is missing: verify that the correct Apple Account is signed in, choose Restore Purchases, and wait for the App Store verification to finish.

A website icon is missing: the site may not provide an accessible icon or may block the request. Tabs Keeper shows a local placeholder; saved titles and links are unaffected.

Contact support

Include your device model, operating system version, Tabs Keeper version, the steps that caused the problem, what you expected, and what happened instead. Attach a screenshot or the exact error message if helpful.

Do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive URLs. For billing or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support because the developer does not receive your full payment information.
