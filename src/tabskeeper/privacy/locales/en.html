Tabs Keeper Privacy Policy

Updated: September 11, 2026

Summary

Tabs Keeper (the “App”) processes most data on your device. The developer does not operate a server that collects tab content, include third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs in the App, or sell your data.

Relevant data is processed by Apple services, websites, or recipients you choose only when you enable iCloud, make a purchase, load a website icon, share content, or send feedback.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Tabs Keeper apps for iOS and macOS and the Safari Web Extension. It explains what data these components process, why they process it, where the data is stored, and how you can manage it.

2. Data Processed by the App

Data TypeDetails and Purpose
Tab dataTab titles, full URLs, website domains, internal identifiers, and sort order, used to save, display, search, share, and reopen tabs. A URL may itself contain information about content you visited.
Organization dataArchive and collection names, tab membership, creation and update times, and Trash status, used to organize and sync your content.
On-device settingsPreferences such as appearance, whether the feature tour has been shown, and the iCloud switch, used to remember your settings.

Feedback you choose to provideWhen you send feedback by email, you choose the email body, attachments, and contact information. The App does not automatically upload this content before you send it.

3. How the Safari Extension Accesses Tabs

To save tabs from the current window, the extension needs permission to access Safari tabs and websites. When you open the extension, it reads the titles, URLs, and available website icon addresses of tabs that can be saved from the current Safari window so it can display a selection list. Only data you confirm for saving is written to the App’s database.

The extension does not read webpage body text, form entries, passwords, or cookies, and it does not continuously record or build a browsing history. You can view, change, or revoke its permissions at any time in Safari’s extension settings. Features that depend on those permissions may stop working after access is revoked.

4. Local Storage and App Group

By default, data is stored as local files in an App Group shared container on your device, allowing the main App and Safari extension to access the same database. Preferences such as appearance, feature-tour status, and the iCloud switch are stored locally on the device.

The developer does not automatically upload this local database to a developer-operated server. Device backups, system migration, and operating-system-level data processing are governed by your system settings and Apple’s services.

5. Optional iCloud Sync

iCloud sync is off by default. When you turn it on, the App merges archives, collections, tabs, and required sync metadata with local data and stores them in the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID, allowing devices signed in to the same Apple ID to sync.

Turning off iCloud sync does not delete local data or automatically remove data previously stored in CloudKit. CloudKit is provided by Apple and is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy and your iCloud settings.

6. AI Smart Select

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the App uses Apple’s on-device system language model only when you tap “AI Smart Select.” The model analyzes the collection name and the titles, domains, and internal identifiers of candidate tabs. The current implementation does not submit full webpage content or send this information to the developer’s server.

AI results are suggestions only. They do not automatically save or delete tabs, or deselect tabs you have already selected. You must still tap the import button to confirm.

7. Website Icon Requests

To display a favicon, the App or Safari may use an icon address supplied by a tab or request /favicon.ico from the corresponding website. The request goes directly to that website, which may receive your IP address, device network information, User-Agent, and similar information sent with a standard network request. The App does not route these requests through the developer’s server. A local placeholder is displayed if the icon cannot be loaded.

8. Pro Purchases and Subscriptions

Apple handles purchases, subscriptions, purchase restoration, and subscription management through the App Store and StoreKit. The App reads product and entitlement status verified by Apple to determine whether Pro is active and to display expiration and renewal information. The developer does not receive your card number, complete payment credentials, or Apple ID password.

9. Sharing and Email Feedback

The App provides selected names, tab titles, and URLs to the system share sheet only when you choose to share them. That data is then processed by the app, service, or contact you select. Sending feedback opens the system email interface, where you review and confirm the message and its contents.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Archives, collections, and tabs are retained until you delete the related content.
  • Archives and collections moved to Trash are retained for 7 days and permanently deleted the next time you open the App after that period. You can also restore or permanently delete them sooner.
  • When a tab no longer belongs to any archive or collection, its record is deleted.
  • To prevent permanently deleted content from reappearing during multi-device merges, the App may retain a minimal deletion marker containing only the item’s internal identifier and deletion time. The marker does not contain tab titles, URLs, or archive or collection names.
  • When iCloud is enabled, local deletions are reflected in CloudKit during a later sync. Uninstalling the App alone may not delete data previously stored in iCloud.

11. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

The developer does not sell or rent your data or use it for targeted advertising. The App does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. To provide features you choose to use, data may be processed by:

  • Apple, which provides iCloud/CloudKit, App Store/StoreKit, system sharing, Mail, and operating-system capabilities.
  • The corresponding website, which receives a standard network request only when a website icon is loaded.
  • Recipients you choose, who receive only content you confirm when sharing or sending email.

12. Security

The App uses Apple platform protections such as app sandboxing, App Groups, system permissions, StoreKit verification, and a private CloudKit database. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security, so keep your device passcode and Apple ID secure.

13. Children’s Privacy

The App does not require you to create an account and does not actively collect age information. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the App. If you believe this has occurred, please contact the developer as described below.

14. Your Choices and Rights

You can revoke Safari extension permissions, turn off iCloud, delete tabs, move archives or collections to Trash and permanently delete them, and manage purchases and subscriptions in system settings. If applicable law gives you rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, you can contact the developer as described below.

15. Policy Updates

If features, data-processing practices, or applicable requirements change materially, the developer will update this policy and revise the date shown above. When appropriate, the developer may also provide notice in the App’s release notes.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or data processing, contact the developer in the App under “Settings” → “Send Feedback by Email.” Do not include passwords, payment information, or other unnecessarily sensitive information in your feedback.
